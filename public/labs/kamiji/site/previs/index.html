      Intended workflow vs what works today
      
    
      Previs is a prototype page in the Kamiji family. It shows the shape of the tool; it is not a finished editor and nothing leaves your browser.
      
    
      
      
      Intended
        
        
      1. Block each scene as a node: pick the cast, a clip, a light, and see it live.
        2. 
        
      2. Wire the nodes into story flow, with branches for alternates and cut or dissolve between scenes.
        3. 
        
      3. Cut shots on the timeline, set camera A and B per shot, write dialogue and notes.
        4. 
        
      4. Hand a printed or exported board to the team, and play the animatic with sound.
        5. 
      
      
      
      Works today
        
        
      • Map: pan, zoom, drag, connect, branch, marquee, minimap, undo. Cards render live under a budget.
        • 
        
      • Timeline: play, scrub, trim, reorder, markers, in and out, 24, 25 or 30 fps.
        • 
        
      • Storyboard with real start-frame renders, and a 6-up print layout.
        • 
        
      • Project saved in this browser; Export and Import JSON.
        • 
      
      
      
      Stubbed or missing
        
        
      • No audio playback: the audio track holds notes only.
        • 
        
      • A dissolve is drawn on the map but plays as a cut.
        • 
        
      • Camera animation is two keyframes per shot; scene animation is the model's own clips.
        • 
        
      • No video export, no user models, no multi-user editing.
        •
