Weather app: plan

Edited 2026-10-27docs/apps/weather.md

Status: plan only, no code. Written 2026-10-27 on task/templeos-shell. Read with docs/holyc-native-apps-plan.md and docs/apps/maps.md (same host-service pattern and renderer gaps).

Evidence tags: [verified] opened or run in this pass; [reported] from a search snippet or secondary source; [memory] unchecked; [estimate] our arithmetic, for the gates to confirm.

1. Purpose and users#

A forecast app for the TempleOS desk: current conditions, the next 48 hours, 10 days, rain timing, alerts, and later radar. It works from a cache with no network. Users: the desk owner with a few saved places. Not in v1: air quality, pollen, history, notifications.

2. Platform facts#

  • No network in the guest [verified, holyc-native-apps-plan]. A host service fetches, caches and normalises; the guest reads a compact record.
  • ASCII text only [verified, Txt and TW, desk3/01-core.HC]. No glyph for °. Write C or F after the number; draw a ring with RRRing if a symbol is needed. Place names are folded to ASCII on the host.
  • Renderer [verified]: rounded rectangles, one-pixel lines (LineN), discs, shadows, Lucide icons (LIcon). A temperature curve needs strokes wider than one pixel, which do not exist (same gap as maps, section 7 there).
  • Icons: Lucide is ISC [verified, assets/LUCIDE-LICENSE.txt].
  • Host [verified locally]: Python 3 with PIL, rsvg-convert, ImageMagick.
  • Kosmos OS [verified, /Users/paulbloch/kosmos-os-frame]: src/os/bento/BentoWeatherWidget.tsx calls api.open-meteo.com from the browser for a fixed city list. No weather app or design exists there. We avoid the direct client call: our data goes through the host with caching, rounding and rate rules.
  • Desk: no weather or Contacts app exists yet [verified].

4. Data sources and terms#

Source Verified terms Verdict
MET Norway (api.met.no) Open data under CC BY 4.0, attribution required [verified]. An identifying User-Agent with app name and contact; generic library strings are blocked [verified]. Cache responses and honour Expires; use If-Modified-Since [verified]. Coordinates at most 4 decimals [verified]. Poll no more than every 10 minutes [verified]. Above 20 requests per second total needs an agreement [verified]. No SLA [verified]. Do not use "Yr", "NRK" or the logo in a service name [verified]. Large animations should be hosted by the site itself [verified] Default global provider. The page we read has no non-commercial clause
Open-Meteo Data CC BY 4.0 [reported]. Free API non-commercial only; under 10,000 calls a day, 5,000 an hour, 600 a minute [reported, official terms, not opened] Personal builds, and the geocoder. Commercial use needs a paid plan or self-hosting
NWS (api.weather.gov) User-Agent required [reported, official page]; an API key is planned to replace it [reported]. Public-domain status not verified US alerts and US detail. Contact value comes from owner settings
Apple WeatherKit 500,000 calls a month per developer membership; paid tiers from US$49.99 for 1 million [reported, developer.apple.com/weatherkit/get-started]. Attribution rules not retrieved Opt-in, for an owner with a membership. Key stays on the host
RainViewer Free API for personal and educational use [reported, rainviewer.com/api.html]. Zoom and rate limits reported inconsistently Personal dev test only
NOAA radar (MRMS, NOWCOAST) Public US data [memory]; terms not checked Radar candidate (P1); check terms at W6

Recommendation: MET Norway as the default, NWS for US alerts and detail, Open-Meteo for the geocoder and personal fallback, WeatherKit opt-in. Each provider is a host adapter with its own rules. The host applies the strictest rule of each: lowest rate, longest cache, and rounding.

Coordinates sent to a provider are rounded to 2 decimals (about 1 km), inside MET's 4-decimal limit. The cache key is the rounded point. Attribution appears in the footer; full credit text goes in the about box.

5. Location and places#

  • Typed city (P0): the host geocodes the text (Open-Meteo's geocoder [memory, check at W4]; Photon from the maps plan offline). The result is a rounded point and an ASCII name.
  • Saved places (P0): up to five, in the host store.
  • Current location (P1): read on the Mac with CoreLocation, after the Mac's prompt and an in-app approval. A plain Python process may not get the prompt [memory]; test with a signed helper at W7.
  • IP location is not used: it is inaccurate and sends the address to a third party.

6. Architecture#

Options: (1) the guest fetches directly, impossible without a network; (2) host service with cache, guest reads a compact record (recommended); (3) host pushes on a timer, which wastes channel time on data nobody asked for. Rejected.

Compact record [estimate, to measure at W1]: current about 16 bytes; 48 hourly entries at about 8 bytes (about 380 bytes); 10 daily entries at about 14 bytes (about 140 bytes); strings about 80 bytes. Total about 0.6 KB, about 13 ms on the 47 KB/s channel [reported figure]. The forecast is cheap; radar is not.

Condition codes map on the host to 12 conditions (clear, mostly clear, cloud, fog, drizzle, rain, sleet, snow, thunder, wind, hail, unknown). Providers differ: Open-Meteo uses WMO codes, MET uses symbol names, NWS uses icon URLs [memory]. The mapping table is tested against fixtures.

Radar: a 128 x 128 mosaic of intensity at 4 bits per cell, 12 frames, run-length coded per row: about 2 to 4 KB per frame, 25 to 50 KB an hour [estimate]. Frames are built on the host from the source, never fetched per user.

Refresh and cache: - Foreground open: cache is used while younger than the provider's Expires (MET) or 15 minutes (others, to check per provider). - Background: the host refreshes saved places at most every 60 minutes while the Mac is awake, within each provider's rules. - Offline: the last record shows with its age ("updated 3 h ago"). - The host counts requests per provider and stops at 80 percent of the lowest documented cap.

Units are stored as tokens and converted in the guest, so a switch never refetches. Dates use English day names, ASCII only, dot as decimal separator.

8. Security and privacy#

  • Location is sensitive. The host asks macOS first; the owner approves in the app second. Nothing is stored before approval.
  • What goes where: settings list, for each provider, the exact rounded coordinates and request type it gets ("MET Norway: 51.51, -0.13, forecast"). Shown before a provider is enabled.
  • Never more precision than the provider allows; never to a provider not enabled.
  • Keys (WeatherKit, any future key) live in a host file with owner-only permissions; the guest never gets them.
  • User-Agent contact comes from owner settings, not hard-coded personal data.
  • Logs hold provider, status and timing; never coordinates unless debug logging is on.
  • Sharing a forecast or forwarding an alert needs a UI prompt.

9. UI specification#

Tokens from browser h1: C_INK, C_MUT, C_CARD, C_BRD, C_ACC for the current value, C_TEAL for rain, C_AMB for sun, C_BAD for alerts. Kosmos OS tokens are not used (primitives file absent).

Rules: keys 1 to 4 switch tabs; C and F switch units. Enter or click a day to show its hours. Alerts sit in a red bar (C_BAD) with event name, end time and "Details" (scrollable full text). Cache older than 6 hours shows its age and a Refresh button, which asks the host and obeys rate rules. Nothing appears that the owner did not ask for.

11. Risks#

  1. Terms conflict: Open-Meteo is non-commercial, RainViewer personal, WeatherKit attribution unread. Mitigation: per-provider rules and separate personal and shipped defaults.
  2. Quality varies by region (MET strong in Europe, NWS in the US). Mitigation: provider shown per place; owner chooses.
  3. QEMU speed on the curve. Mitigation: fewer points; 24-hour default.
  4. ASCII limits (no °, no accents). Mitigation: C, F, folded names.
  5. macOS location may need a signed helper. Mitigation: P1, tested at W7.
  6. Radar terms unchecked. Mitigation: no radar until W6 review.
  7. Stale data shown as current. Mitigation: age always visible.

12. Effort#

P0 (W0 to W4, host daemon, stroke renderer work): about M to L, 5 to 7 weeks for one developer. P1 (radar, alerts polish, widget, location) adds 4 to 6 weeks. The host daemon can start before the renderer work and be tested alone.

Verified: desk renderer and font code; Kosmos OS bento widget's direct Open-Meteo call; MET Norway terms (fetched); Photon, Lucide, CoMaps and earcut licence files (used in the maps plan); the Mac's tools.
