Weather and Music prototype: design notes

Edited 2026-10-27docs/apps/weather-music-prototype.md

Hub: apps-host/hub/hostd.py owns the one VM serial connection, routes by service (echo, time, weather, music, sys), forwards GET lines to scripts/browser-bridge.py (imported, not edited). Guest: desk3/03h-hostlink.HC (transport, HostCall), 03k-hostpoll.HC (HostPoll, after the apps because HolyC does not reliably resolve calls to later-defined functions), 03i-weather.HC, 03j-music.HC.

afplay limits (honest)#

No seek, no pause, no live volume. We use SIGSTOP/SIGCONT on our own pid for pause (queued audio drains, so sound can trail by a fraction of a second); seek and volume change restart afplay on a trimmed temp WAV (python wave or ffmpeg), with a 100 to 200 ms gap. Position is a host clock, not the device clock; spectrum is computed from the file at that position, not captured.

Next step: Swift AVAudioEngine helper#

A small Swift binary the hub starts and talks to over a Unix socket in JSON lines: load, play, pause, seek, volume, state at 10 Hz, 16 FFT bands from an output tap (Accelerate). Gapless by scheduling the next file with 5 s left; formats AVFoundation lacks go through ffmpeg to a temp WAV. The service keeps its op names, so the guest does not change.
