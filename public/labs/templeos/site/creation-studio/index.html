How it works

HolyC on the surface. Strong engines underneath.

The windows you use are native HolyC, so they look and feel like the desktop. The heavy lifting, such as decoding a PSD or rendering a PDF page, runs in open-source engines on the host, reached through a permissioned service layer.

Tech specs.

Engines

Creation Studio builds on a family of open-source creative apps whose engines run as separate programs. They are early alpha and move fast, so we pin a release by checksum and expect breakage. Code is MIT or Apache-2.0. Their names and logos are trademarks and stay out of our builds.

Routes

Our preferred route is the command-line and tool interfaces of those engines, run as host services behind the hub, feeding native HolyC windows. Photo and Vector currently run as streamed windows, which works but is slower over the serial link.

Limits today

No audio or video playback in the guest. Some engines need a download we ask you to approve. Generative image features are not part of the engines we use.

Formats

xlsx, xlsb, ods, docx, odt, rtf, md, pptx, pdf, svg, psd, webp, exr, heic and more, depending on the engine. Support varies by tool and is still being tested.
