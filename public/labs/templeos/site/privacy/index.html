Privacy

It asks. It does not assume.

Your files stay on your machine. When something has to go out, you can see which app sends it and where it goes.

Describes the design and the code in the repository. The guest side is not independently audited.

Security

How a request works.

The same four steps for every app and service.

  1. An app asks

    It names a permission word, such as one folder or one site.

  2. The host checks

    Grants live on your Mac, not in the guest. Default is no.

  3. The desktop asks you

    A sheet shows who is asking and what for. Decisions are tied to that app, version and package.

  4. One use, then gone

    The answer is a one-use token. More permissions in an update are asked again.
