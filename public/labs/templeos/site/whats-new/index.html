What's new

Everything that changed.

Newest first. There are no numbered releases yet, so entries are dated by when they landed.

  1. 2026-10-27WalkieVoice dictation and prompts in a desktop window, backed by a host service. Work in progress.
  2. 2026-10-27Faster text and canvas prototypeA headless host streams drawing meshes that a HolyC rasteriser paints at native density.
  3. 2026-10-27Photo and Vector windowsTwo creative apps from the open-source ArtCraft family, streamed into the desktop as tiles.
  4. 2026-10-27Fonts, version 2A new text engine, six families, a Fonts page in Settings and a native Font Book.
  5. 2026-10-27SheetA spreadsheet window with a formula bar, tabs, sample sheets and CSV save.
  6. 2026-10-27Kamiji UI kit, Components and KamikinA component showcase in every skin and a chat with real animated avatars at 24 frames a second.
  7. 2026-10-27Top bar styleGlass, solid, tinted or transparent; always shown or auto-hide; ink that adapts to the wallpaper.
  8. 2026-10-27Browser, version 1Pages rendered by Chrome on the host and shown through the hub, with fixes to Kamiji and Allow.
  9. 2026-10-27Installer designA .ksapp format with signing, trust states and quarantine, plus a host-side prototype.
  10. 2026-10-27Poster and wallpapersA poster layer, a wallpaper service and a photo fetch tool with credits.
  11. 2026-10-27Calendar, Clock, Weather, MusicFour everyday apps backed by host services.
  12. 2026-10-27Boot splashA ring, a bar and a crossfade from a saved image. The TempleOS terminal never shows.
  13. 2026-10-27Renderer passDragging a large window fell from 72.1 ms to 9.5 ms a frame.
  14. 2026-10-27Native arm64 buildThe whole desktop runs on Apple Silicon through Aiwnios, with no emulator.
  15. 2026-10-26Custom-drawn desktopThe first port of the themes: a menubar, rounded windows and a dock in 16 colours.

From the repository log. For the longer story see History and Versions.
