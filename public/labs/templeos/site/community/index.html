Native Messenger

Talk from inside the desktop.

One window for every network, with one store on your Mac and the networks behind a provider interface. The desktop only draws it and sends what you typed. Your keys and accounts stay on the host.

Sending is always your click, and a real account asks for approval the first time it syncs or sends.

A native window with conversations, filters, unread badges, bubbles and a composer, over a host store. A scripted sample provider shows the whole path, with 19 service tests.
