Method

How the schedule is built

  • Done bars span the first to the last commit that touched the ticket's source file.
  • In progress work starts at its first commit and ends after half its estimate, at least three working days from today.
  • Planned tickets are queued one worker per area in plan order, after their dependencies, using the plan's estimate where it has one and 5 working days where it does not (the detail says which).
  • Proposed tickets are ideas from research that nobody has approved; they queue after the planned work in their area.
  • Dropped tickets have no bar.

The source is data/roadmap-tickets.json; scripts/gen-roadmap.py writes this page's data and docs/roadmap.md. Edit the tickets, rerun the script, rebuild the site.

Pick a ticket.

Every ticket links to the note it came from, and most are small enough to take in a week.
