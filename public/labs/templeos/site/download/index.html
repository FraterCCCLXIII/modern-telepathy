Which one?

Native, for arm64

A window on your desktop, no emulator. About half a second to the desktop on an M-series Mac, and fast window drags.

Classic, for x86

The original TempleOS image in QEMU, with everything underneath it. Slower where it has to emulate x86.

In your browser

No install: a sketch of the desktop that runs in this page, on any device. Try it.
